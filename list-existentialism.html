Existentialism

Book

The Myth of Sisyphus

Albert Camus · 1942

Essay

A Free Man's Worship

Bertrand Russell · 1903

Essay

On the Suffering of the World

Arthur Schopenhauer · 1851

Essay

My Confession

Leo Tolstoy · 1882

Book

Being and Nothingness

Jean-Paul Sartre · 1943

Sartre’s magnum opus. Dense but indispensable for understanding bad faith, radical freedom, and being-for-others.

Short Story

“The Wall”

Jean-Paul Sartre · 1939

A condemned prisoner awaits execution. Sartre distills the existential confrontation with death into a single devastating night.

Novel

Notes from Underground

Fyodor Dostoevsky · 1864

Often cited as the first existentialist novel. The Underground Man’s bitter monologue anticipates nearly every major theme of the movement.

Film

The Seventh Seal

Ingmar Bergman · 1957

A knight plays chess with Death. Bergman’s allegory on faith, silence, and the search for meaning in a godless world.

Film

Ikiru

Akira Kurosawa · 1952

A dying bureaucrat discovers purpose too late. One of the most compassionate existentialist works in any medium.

Play

Waiting for Godot

Samuel Beckett · 1953

Two men wait for someone who never comes. Beckett makes the absurdity of existence not just philosophical but theatrical.

Essay

“Existentialism Is a Humanism”

Jean-Paul Sartre · 1946

Sartre’s public lecture defending existentialism against its critics. The most accessible entry point into his philosophy.
